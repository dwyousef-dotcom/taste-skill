The things people ask first

Is this actually free?
Yes. You never give us a card, a budget, or a signature, and you are never billed for the ads. We pay that bill because we want the case study. The only thing we ask for is a review at the end, and only if the pilot worked for you.
What happens after the 10 inquiries?
We stop, and you decide. If you want to keep going, it's paid out of what the ads bring in, and by then you'll know exactly what an inquiry costs you because every one of them was tracked. If you'd rather stop, you keep the patients you booked and we part ways. There is nothing to cancel.
Where do these patients come from?
People near you searching for a Yellow Fever or travel-vaccine clinic before a trip. They are already looking; we just make sure your counter is what they find.
What if I don't stock the vaccine?
You don't need it on the shelf. Answer the call, book the patient a few days out, and order the vaccine in for the appointment.
What do I say on the call?
We send you a one-page guide before anything goes live: what to confirm, what to say, how to book. We also send a recommended price range for your area so you're not guessing.
Does this touch my website, POS, or accounts?
No. Everything we build stands on its own, away from your systems. The only thing that reaches you is the phone call. And if you don't have a website at all, it makes no difference.
Why only one pharmacy per city?
Because two pharmacies in the same city would be bidding against each other on the same searches, which pushes the cost up and splits the patients. One per city keeps the inquiries yours alone.
What's the next step?
Book a 15-minute call, or start the setup here on the site. Either way we confirm your city is open before anything launches.
