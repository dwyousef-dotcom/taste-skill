Quick answers

Where do these patients come from?

They're real, urgent travelers searching right now for a certified travel-vaccine clinic before a trip. We reach them at a much lower cost per inquiry and connect that demand straight to you.

What if I don't stock the vaccine?

You don't need to keep it on hand. Answer the call, book the patient two or three days out, and order the vaccine in for the appointment. Plenty of time.

How should I price it?

The moment you're in, we send a short report with a recommended price range for your area, not too low and not too high, so you're never guessing.

What do I actually say on the call?

We send you a simple one-page guide for handling a travel-vaccine inquiry: what to confirm, what to say, how to book. Nothing complicated.

Does this touch my website, POS, or accounts?

No. Everything we build is external and standalone. It doesn't connect to your website, your point-of-sale, or any of your accounts. The only thing that reaches you is the phone call.

What if I don't have a website?

Doesn't matter at all. We build the entire thing separately: the page, the ads, the routing. You don't need anything of your own.

Why are you doing this for free?

We're expanding city by city and need real pharmacies to build winning case studies with. You get free patients; we get a result we can show the next city. That case study is worth far more to us than the cost of the pilot.

I didn't get an email. Can I still join?

You can put your clinic forward. Places are limited and we pre-filter against the bar above, but if you're a good fit we want to hear from you. Request an invite below.
